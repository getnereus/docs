    References¶

    Every technique, method, practice, quote, book, author, film, paper and reference that the project and its predecessors mention, harvested on 2026-10-27 to show how much context surrounds a project, and to credit it (Credit what we use, #332). The owner (Silvio) suggested most of them; the evidence below credits each one only where a file says who brought it, so many stay unknown.

    How this list was made¶

    Six read-only agents harvested one source each and a script merged their rows by name; three more agents checked the books, papers, quotes, myths and the most repeated items on the web. The sources:

    • nereus: Nereus repository.
    • wasp-idp-docs: wasp-idp (docs/).
    • wasp-idp-rest: wasp-idp (code, commits, issues).
    • platform-api: platform-api (the former name of Nereus).
    • early-platform-api: early-platform-api (a private earlier copy).
    • drive: raw sessions on Drive (#pontos/raw).

    The columns:

    • Author / origin and Year: as the source states them; when the web check found different values, the Verified column says so.
    • Brought by: who put it into the conversation, from the evidence (a session file that marks who said what, a "Credit" section, an issue label). unknown means the only evidence is a commit by Silvio co-authored by Claude, which does not say who proposed the idea.
    • Verified: yes with the page opened; known, page not opened when the checker recognised a classic but did not open the page; differs when the page gives another author or year; checked in the session when the raw session says it was checked then; linked in the source, not opened; project practice for the project's own rules, which have nothing outside to check; not confirmed on the web when the check could not open or find the text (often a quote inside a book or a report); not checked.
    • Where: the first places it appears, <source>:<path>:<line>; (+N) counts the other places.
